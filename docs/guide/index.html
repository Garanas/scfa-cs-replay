The map editor: a guide

A guide to the FAF map editor's window, made from the editor's own tours. Help, Tours in the editor plays each one over the window.

  • A tour of the window
    The parts of the editor's window, in about two minutes.
  • Drafting a map
    Sketch a map before you build it: pencil strokes, symbols, labels, sheets and a picture to trace.
  • Shaping the terrain
    Sculpt the ground in layers you can change later: tools, brushes, height layers and undo.
  • Masks: where things may happen
    Make a mask of your own, paint it, build it from layers and rules, and see where it is used.
  • Painting the terrain's strata
    The terrain's textures: strata, their masks, the strata brush and the 3D view.
  • A tour of the Markers mode
    Spawns, mass and hydrocarbon deposits, path nodes and chains, with one deposit placed by you.
  • A tour of the Units mode
    Armies, groups and units: the palette, placing one unit, placing many, and presets.
  • A tour of the Procedural mode and the export
    Results the editor works out from the map, the steps of the export, and where to find Export and Upload to the vault.

Made with the map editor 1.0.0+0b2734474d350fcba68c3bdae8a6574fef65815c on 2026-10-13, window 1280 by 800, dark theme.
